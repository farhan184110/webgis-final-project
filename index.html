About HomeScope Tempe

HomeScope Tempe is a student-developed WebGIS dashboard for exploring housing costs, population characteristics, and nearby amenities in Tempe, Arizona.

Use the map to explore census block groups, compare housing indicators, and locate transit stops, schools, grocery stores, healthcare facilities, and parks.

Understanding the information

Census block groups are statistical areas and do not necessarily represent neighborhood boundaries. Area-level statistics describe the block group as a whole, not individual homes or residents.

Nearby amenities are measured within a one-mile straight-line radius of the selected block group's centroid. This does not represent walking distance or travel time.

This dashboard is a work in progress. Any sample values are provided for demonstration and should not be used for housing decisions.
