HomeScope Tempe User Guide

Explore Housing, People, and Nearby Amenities in Tempe

1. Introduction

HomeScope Tempe is an interactive WebGIS dashboard designed to help users explore housing costs, population characteristics, and nearby amenities in Tempe, Arizona.

The dashboard allows residents, students, prospective homebuyers, renters, and other interested users to compare housing and neighborhood characteristics based on their preferences.

Using HomeScope Tempe, users can:

  • Explore median monthly rent and home values.
  • Filter census block groups based on housing preferences.
  • View household income and population characteristics.
  • Locate transit stops, schools, grocery stores, hospitals, clinics, and parks.
  • Explore nearby amenities using an interactive map.

What is a Census Block Group?

A census block group is a small geographic area defined by the U.S. Census Bureau for collecting and reporting statistical information. HomeScope Tempe uses census block groups to display housing, income, and population information.

The information represents the census block group as a whole and does not describe individual properties or households.

2. Navigating the Website

The navigation bar is located at the top of the HomeScope Tempe dashboard. It provides access to the interactive map, project information, data sources, and team contact details.

2.1 HomeScope Tempe Logo

The HomeScope Tempe logo and project name are located in the upper-left corner of the navigation bar. Click the logo or project name to return to the main dashboard.

2.2 Map

The Map option provides access to the main interactive dashboard, where users can filter census block groups, explore housing costs, view population characteristics, and locate nearby amenities.

Click Map in the upper-right corner of the navigation bar to open the main dashboard.

2.3 About

Click About in the top navigation bar to open the About HomeScope Tempe window. This window explains the purpose of the dashboard and provides guidance on interpreting the information.

The About window provides information on:

  • The purpose of HomeScope Tempe.
  • Housing, population, and amenity information.
  • Understanding census block group statistics.
  • How nearby amenities are measured.
  • Limitations of the dashboard.

To close the window and return to the interactive map, click the × button in the upper-right corner of the pop-up window.

2.4 Data

Click Data in the top navigation bar to open the Data Sources window. This window identifies the organizations and agencies that provide the data displayed in HomeScope Tempe.

The Data Sources window includes information on:

  • Housing and demographic data.
  • Transit stops.
  • Schools.
  • Grocery stores.
  • Hospitals and clinics.
  • Parks.
  • Basemaps.
  • Data limitations.

For more information about the datasets and their limitations, refer to Section 7: Understanding the Data of this user guide.

To close the Data Sources window, click the × button in the upper-right corner.

2.5 Contact

Click Contact in the top navigation bar to open the Contact the Team window. This window provides contact information for the HomeScope Tempe project team.

Users can contact the team to:

  • Ask questions about the dashboard.
  • Provide feedback and suggestions.
  • Report missing or incorrect information.
  • Request clarification about the project.

Click a team member's email address in the Contact window to open your email application and send a message.

For more information about the team members and their contributions, refer to Section 8: Project Team of this user guide.

To close the Contact window, click the × button in the upper-right corner.

3. Dashboard Overview

The HomeScope Tempe dashboard is organized into three main panels. Each panel provides different tools and information to help users explore housing and neighborhood characteristics.

3.1 Left Panel — Filter Census Block Groups

The left panel allows users to filter census block groups based on median monthly rent and median home value. Users can also choose which amenities to display on the map.

For detailed instructions, refer to Section 4: Filter Census Block Groups.

3.2 Center Panel — Explore Tempe Housing

The center panel contains the interactive map of Tempe. Users can zoom, move around the map, explore census block groups, change basemaps, and view amenity locations.

For detailed instructions, refer to Section 5: Explore Tempe Housing.

3.3 Right Panel — Selected Census Block Group

The right panel displays information about the census block group selected on the map, including housing costs, household income, population characteristics, age distribution, and nearby amenities.

For detailed instructions, refer to Section 6: Selected Census Block Group.

4. Filter Census Block Groups

The filter panel is located on the left side of the HomeScope Tempe dashboard. It allows users to identify census block groups based on their preferred monthly rent and home value ranges. Users can also choose which amenities to display on the interactive map.

The filter panel contains five main components:

  1. Monthly Rent (Median)
  2. Home Value (Median)
  3. Amenities to Show on Map
  4. Apply Filters
  5. Reset Filters

In the example shown in Figure 6, a monthly rent range of $1,500 to $2,000 has been applied. The result indicates that 52 of the 119 census block groups match the selected housing criteria. All five amenity layers are selected for display on the map.

4.1 Monthly Rent

The Monthly Rent filter allows users to identify census block groups within their preferred median monthly rent range.

How to use this filter:

  1. Check the Filter by monthly rent checkbox.
  2. Enter the desired minimum monthly rent.
  3. Enter the desired maximum monthly rent.
  4. Click Apply Filters to view matching census block groups on the map.

In Figure 7, the selected monthly rent range is $1,000 to $2,000.

Uncheck the Filter by monthly rent checkbox if you do not want to filter by rent, and click Apply Filters.

Note: Median monthly rent represents a census block group-level statistic, not the actual rental price of an individual property.

4.2 Home Value

The Home Value filter allows users to identify census block groups within their preferred median home value range.

How to use this filter:

  1. Check the Filter by home value checkbox.
  2. Enter the desired minimum home value.
  3. Enter the desired maximum home value.
  4. Click Apply Filters to view matching census block groups on the map.

In Figure 8, the selected home value range is $200,000 to $500,000.

Uncheck the Filter by home value checkbox if you do not want to filter by home value, and click Apply Filters.

Note: Median home value represents a census block group-level statistic, not the actual sale price of an individual property.

4.3 Amenities to Show on Map

The Amenities to Show on Map section allows users to choose which types of facilities and services to display on the interactive map.

Available amenity layers:

  • Transit Stops: Bus stops and light rail stations.
  • Schools: School locations.
  • Grocery Stores: Locations of grocery stores.
  • Hospitals & Clinics: Healthcare facilities.
  • Parks: Park locations.

How to display amenities:

  1. Check the checkbox next to the desired amenity category.
  2. Select multiple categories to explore different amenities together.
  3. Uncheck a category to hide its locations from the map.

In Figure 9, all five amenity categories are selected for display on the map.

4.4 Apply Filters

The Apply Filters button is located at the bottom of the left panel. It updates the map based on the selected housing criteria.

How to apply filters:

  1. Select the desired housing filters.
  2. Enter the minimum and maximum values.
  3. Click the Apply Filters button.
  4. Review the matching census block groups on the interactive map.

Understanding the results:

The message below the filter buttons indicates how many census block groups match the selected housing criteria out of the total number of block groups included in the dashboard.

Users can modify their housing preferences and click Apply Filters again to update the results.

4.5 Reset Filters

The Reset Filters button is located below the Apply Filters button, as shown in Figure 10. It allows users to clear their current filter selections and begin a new housing search.

How to reset filters:

  1. Click the Reset Filters button.
  2. Review the updated filter settings and map.
  3. Select new housing preferences and amenity categories as needed.
  4. Click Apply Filters to begin a new search.

This feature is useful when users want to explore different housing price ranges or compare different housing preferences.

5. Explore Tempe Housing

The Explore Tempe Housing panel is located in the center of the HomeScope Tempe dashboard. It contains an interactive map that allows users to explore census block groups, visualize housing information, and locate nearby amenities.

Users can move around the map, zoom in and out, change basemaps, and select individual census block groups to view detailed information.

The interactive map contains seven main components:

  1. Map Navigation
  2. Zoom Controls
  3. Basemap and Layer Controls
  4. Map Colors
  5. Amenity Icons and Clusters
  6. Selecting a Census Block Group
  7. Map Scale and Places in View

Each of these components is explained in the following subsections.

5.1 Map Navigation

The interactive map allows users to explore different areas of Tempe and its surrounding areas. Users can move the map in any direction to examine census block groups and nearby amenities.

How to navigate the map:

  1. Place your mouse cursor anywhere on the map.
  2. Click and hold the left mouse button.
  3. Drag the map in the desired direction.
  4. Release the mouse button when you reach the area you want to explore.

Moving the map allows users to explore different locations without changing their selected housing filters.

5.2 Zoom Controls

The zoom controls are located in the upper-left corner of the interactive map. They allow users to change the level of detail displayed on the map.

How to use the zoom controls:

  • Zoom In (+): Click the plus button to view a smaller geographic area in greater detail.
  • Zoom Out (-): Click the minus button to view a larger geographic area.

Users can also use the mouse scroll wheel to zoom in and out of the map.

Zooming in helps users explore individual census block groups and amenity locations, while zooming out provides a broader view of Tempe and its surrounding areas.

5.3 Basemap and Layer Controls

The basemap control is located below the zoom buttons in the upper-left corner of the map. It allows users to change the background map according to their viewing preferences.

Available basemaps:

  • OpenStreetMap: Displays streets, buildings, landmarks, and other geographic features for general map exploration.
  • USGS Topo: Displays topographic information, terrain, elevation contours, and geographic features.

How to change the basemap:

  1. Move your mouse over the Layers icon.
  2. Select OpenStreetMap or USGS Topo from the expanded menu.
  3. View the updated background map.

Changing the basemap changes the background appearance without changing the selected housing filters or amenity layers.

5.4 Amenity Icons and Clusters

Amenities are displayed on the interactive map using distinct icons and geographic features. Users can show or hide different amenity categories using the checkboxes in the left-side filter panel.

Understanding Map Symbols

  • Individual Icons: Represent individual amenity locations, such as bus stops, light rail stations, schools, grocery stores, hospitals, and clinics.
  • Numbered Clusters: Represent multiple nearby amenity markers grouped together to reduce map congestion. The number indicates how many markers are included in that cluster.
  • Park Boundaries: Green shaded areas represent park locations and their geographic boundaries.

How to Explore Amenities

  1. Enable the desired amenity categories using the checkboxes in the left panel.
  2. Zoom in or click a numbered cluster to explore the individual amenity locations.
  3. Hover over an individual amenity to view its name.
  4. Click an individual amenity to open a popup containing additional information.

Marker clusters automatically adjust as users zoom in or out, making it easier to explore amenities at different geographic scales.

5.5 Selecting a Census Block Group

Users can select individual census block groups on the interactive map to explore their housing costs, household income, demographic characteristics, and nearby amenities.

How to select a census block group:

  1. Navigate to the area of interest on the interactive map.
  2. Apply the preferred housing filters, if needed, to identify matching census block groups.
  3. Click directly on a census block group to select it.
  4. The selected census block group boundary will be highlighted on the map.
  5. View the corresponding housing, demographic, and nearby amenity information in the right-side panel.
  6. Click another census block group to explore and compare its information.

For a detailed explanation of the information displayed in the right-side panel, refer to Section 6: Selected Census Block Group .

6. Selected Census Block Group

The right-side information panel displays housing, income, demographic, and nearby amenity information for the selected census block group. Click on a census block group on the interactive map to view its details. The information automatically updates when a different block group is selected.

6.1 Housing and Income Details

This section provides an overview of housing costs and household income within the selected census block group.

  • Median Monthly Rent: The median monthly rent reported for the census block group.
  • Median Home Value: The median reported value of owner-occupied housing units.
  • Median Household Income: The median annual income of households within the census block group.

Users can compare these values across different census block groups to explore housing costs and income conditions.

6.2 Who Lives Here?

This section describes the population and housing occupancy characteristics of the selected census block group.

  • Population: The total estimated number of residents.
  • Median Age: The age that divides the population into two equal halves.
  • Renter-Occupied Homes: The number and percentage of occupied housing units occupied by renters.
  • Owner-Occupied Homes: The number and percentage of occupied housing units occupied by their owners.

6.3 Age Distribution

The horizontal stacked bar chart shows the percentage of residents belonging to four age groups:

  • Under 18: Children and adolescents.
  • 18-39: Adults aged 18 to 39.
  • 40-64: Adults aged 40 to 64.
  • 65+: Adults aged 65 and older.

Each colored segment represents the proportion of the total population within that age group. Larger segments indicate higher population shares.

6.4 Nearby Amenities

This section displays the number of amenities located within a one-mile radius of the selected census block group's centroid (geographic center).

  • Transit Stops: Total bus stops and light rail stations, with separate counts for each type.
  • Schools: Number of school locations.
  • Grocery Stores: Number of grocery store locations.
  • Hospitals & Clinics: Number of healthcare facility locations.
  • Parks: Number of nearby parks.

Note: The one-mile distance is measured as a straight-line distance from the block group centroid. It does not represent actual walking distance, driving distance, or travel time.

7.2 Methodology

HomeScope Tempe combines housing, demographic, and amenity data to help users explore housing affordability and neighborhood characteristics across Tempe.

Geographic Unit of Analysis

Census block groups are used as the primary geographic unit of analysis. Housing and demographic information is associated with each census block group using its unique geographic identifier (GEOID).

Housing Affordability Filters

Users can identify census block groups based on their preferred monthly rent and home value ranges. When both filters are enabled, a block group must satisfy both criteria to match the selected filters. The dashboard displays the number of matching census block groups.

Nearby Amenity Analysis

Nearby amenities are identified using a one-mile radius around the centroid of each census block group. The number of transit stops, schools, grocery stores, hospitals and clinics, and parks within this distance is displayed in the selected block group information panel.

The one-mile radius represents straight-line distance rather than distance along a road or pedestrian network.

Interactive Map Visualization

Geographic data is displayed using GeoJSON layers and the Leaflet JavaScript mapping library. Users can interact with census block groups, control amenity visibility, change basemaps, and explore housing and demographic information through the interactive dashboard.

7.3 Limitations

HomeScope Tempe is designed as an exploratory tool for understanding housing affordability and neighborhood characteristics. Users should consider the following limitations when interpreting the information.

  • Geographic Scale: Housing and demographic information is reported at the census block group level and does not represent individual households or properties.
  • Housing Cost Estimates: Median monthly rent and median home value are Census estimates and may differ from current rental listings and property prices.
  • Data Availability: Some census block groups may have missing estimates. Data from different sources may also represent different collection periods.
  • Amenity Accessibility: Nearby amenities are measured within a one-mile radius of the census block group centroid. This does not account for road networks, walking routes, physical barriers, or travel time.
  • Amenity Characteristics: The number of nearby amenities does not necessarily indicate their quality, capacity, affordability, or availability of services.
  • Housing Affordability: The dashboard allows users to explore housing costs but does not calculate individual household affordability or include all living expenses, such as utilities and transportation costs.
  • Decision-Making: The dashboard is intended for informational and educational purposes. Users should verify current housing prices and local conditions before making housing decisions.
